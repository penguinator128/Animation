ASTRIS PRODUCTIONS: supporting lines
====================================
Twelve options for the tiny line that sits under the name. All lowercase, all ending in a full stop,
all meant to feel like an invitation in. Swap the one each style uses in brand-config.json ("tagline").

 1. welcome in.                    <- used by STYLE 1: RED
 2. pull up a chair.               <- used by STYLE 2: YELLOW
 3. stories, slowly.
 4. made with friends.             <- used by STYLE 3: WILDCARD
 5. small films, big feelings.
 6. stay for the credits.
 7. you're just in time.
 8. films made by hand.
 9. come see what we made.
10. make yourself at home.
11. bring snacks.
12. good stories, good company.

Why these three:
  RED       "welcome in."        the warmest and shortest. It reads like a hand on the door.
  YELLOW    "pull up a chair."   sunny and confident: an invitation with a smile.
  WILDCARD  "made with friends." fits the handmade, zine feel of the two-ink print.
