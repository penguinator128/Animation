# ASTRIS website prototype, version 3

The third clickable draft. Nothing here is on the live website. Double-click `index.html` to open it.
Earlier drafts are in `../prototype/` and `../prototype-2/` so you can compare.

## What changed from version 2

- **New intro (about 4 seconds):** the bar slams down the A's slant, a burst of light goes off, the A's film strip rips past your latest work (fast, then slowing) with its sprocket holes moving, then the camera zooms into the last frame and the site arrives. Click to skip.
- **Film grain** over the whole site (a little stronger on the home screen).
- **Five page transitions that take turns:** a film reel sweeping left to right, one sweeping back, slanted yellow bars, a clapperboard closing, and an iris. The film reel's frames and sprocket holes scroll as it passes.
- **Footer reel:** it just rolls on its own now. No arrows or dragging.
- **Footer and intro say "Josh"** (not Josh Brogan). The YouTube handle is @AstrisProductions128. Instagram and Letterboxd are left off until you have them.

## Your showreel

Save your video (about one minute is perfect) as `showreel.mp4` next to `index.html`. It plays silent, on a loop, behind every page. Until then a slideshow of stills stands in. Keep the file under about 25 MB (1080p, H.264) so it starts quickly.
