# ASTRIS website prototype

A clickable mini version of the new site. Nothing here is on the live website.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html` (it opens in your browser). The pages are Home, Films, Photography, Work in progress, News and Say hello.

## Your showreel

Put your video in this folder and name it **`showreel.mp4`** (next to `index.html`). It then plays silent, on a loop, behind every page.
Until it is there, a slow slideshow of stills stands in for it. A short, compressed file (under 20 MB, 1080p) loads fastest.

## What to try

- **Menu on the left.** Click any page. The reel keeps playing behind it.
- **Films:** sort by date, length or title, filter by type, and flip between the list and the film strip. Click a film to flip through its frames.
- **Photography:** filter by black and white, macro or free to download; pick a project; sort. Click a photo for the full-size view and the download button.
- **Work in progress:** see how far each project is. News: filter by topic.
- Arrow keys step through photos and film frames. Escape closes things.

## Sample details

Film titles come from `films.txt`. Dates, types, project names and anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS] are examples, so you can see how sorting works. Photo titles are placeholders. The photos are your own.
