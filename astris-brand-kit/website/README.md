# website: ideas for the new ASTRIS site

Draft pictures only, no code yet. Nothing here has been put on the live website or pushed to the website repo.
Start with [`astris-website-concepts-overview.jpg`](astris-website-concepts-overview.jpg) (all ten on one sheet).

## The concepts (`ui-concepts/`)

| # | Concept | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Showreel hero**: your showreel plays full screen behind a big name, with a project list down the left and a scroll cue | The homepage |
| 02 | **Contact sheet**: a numbered grid with bracketed cells and yellow numbers | The films page |
| 03 | **Big type poster**: a giant name on yellow with overlapping prints and hard shadows | A bold homepage, or the "about" intro |
| 04 | **Index list**: a big confident list of films; the still follows your cursor | The films page |
| 05 | **Split screen**: a fixed yellow panel beside a scrolling wall of stills | Films or photos |
| 06 | **Marquee tape and stickers**: loud, friendly, a little cheeky | The homepage, or the news page |
| 07 | **Film strip gallery**: a tilted strip of frames that scrolls sideways | The films page |
| 08 | **Say hello**: your silhouette picture and one big email link | About and contact |
| 09 | **On a phone**: the showreel home, the full-screen menu and the films page | All pages, small screens |
| 10 | **Astris Studio**: the back end, bright yellow only where you need to act | The Studio (admin) |

## Things to know

- **The photos are stand-ins.** They are your own pictures from the website repo. The showreel in 01 and 09 will be your real video.
- **Words in [SQUARE BRACKETS] are placeholders**, same as the rest of the kit. The film titles are from `films.txt`.
- **Colours and type** are the Yellow style from the kit: Golden Yellow `#F2B705`, Near Black `#141210`, Warm Off-White `#F2EBDD`; Anton for big lines, Inter for small text.
- These are drafts to mix and match. Say which parts you like ("the list from 04, the strip from 07") and they can be combined.
