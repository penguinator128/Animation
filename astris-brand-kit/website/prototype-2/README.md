# ASTRIS website prototype, version 2

The second clickable draft. Nothing here is on the live website. Double-click `index.html` to open it.
(`../prototype/` is the first draft, kept so you can compare.)

## What changed from the first draft

- **Home:** a centred ASTRIS PRODUCTIONS over the showreel, one button, and a scroll cue. No timer, no side text.
- **Footer on every page:** the site links, contact details and, at the very bottom, the scrolling film reel. Drag it, use the arrows, or let it roll by itself.
- **Films:** one gallery. Finished films and in-progress films sit together; the in-progress ones have a banner and a progress bar. Filter by All, Finished or In progress, by type, and sort.
- **News posts:** click a post to read it, with a photo gallery that opens full size.
- **Page changes:** a strip of film wipes across the screen.
- **Intro:** shorter (about 2 seconds) and smaller.
- **Your details:** name, email, location and what you do come from your current website. Handles and anything in [brackets] are still to fill in.

## Your showreel

Save your video as `showreel.mp4` next to `index.html`. It plays silent, on a loop, behind every page. Until then a slideshow of stills stands in.

## Also in here (prototype only)

- `index.html#studio`: a preview of how the separate Studio site could work, with a "Feature on intro" switch for each film and photo. The Studio will be its own website on the real build.
